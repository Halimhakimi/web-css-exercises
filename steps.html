How I Plan to Become a Graphic Designer

  1. Earn a Bachelor's Degree in Graphic Design or Visual Arts
  2. Master Adobe Creative Suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign)
  3. Create a strong personal portfolio
  4. Complete internships or freelance projects
  5. Join online communities and attend design workshops
  6. Apply for junior designer roles and keep improving
